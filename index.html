COURSE CALCULATOR

Course Grade Calculator

Your course grade is calculated as a weighted average of your exams/assignments from this course. "Weighted" means that some exams/assignments may have a bigger impact on your final grade than others. It's important that you check how heavily weighted each exam/assignment is in your course as this can create a big difference :)) For instance, for the course "Research Methods and Statistics (RMS)", there are 4 exams, each worth 25% of your grade. Failing one exam in this course may not have a significant impact on your final grade. This is different from, for example, Developmental Psychology, where there is only one exam worth 100% of your final course grade.

ACADEMIC YEAR TRACKER

Academic Year Calculator

List every course from your academic year with its grade and EC (credits). Your academic year grade is calculated by 1) Multiplying each course's grade by its EC, 2) Summing these products together, and 3) Dividing by the total EC you can obtain during your academic year. This means that courses which have a smaller weight (e.g., 3 EC) will have less impact on your final grade.

How was the calculator made?

Examination Regulations:

This calculator is based on the Examinations Regulations Year 1 2025–2026.pdf (p.6-8), as specified by the Teaching and Examination Regulations for Psychology @ University of Amsterdam. Please note that this is a simplified version based on examination regulations from 2025-2026, and therefore may not reflect the most current updates. It is highly recommended to consult the most recent Examinations Regulations for your grade calculation, as well as verify the results you get from CalPsych with the official calculator given by the University of Amsterdam. The developer of CalPsych is not held responsible for the users' grades. Hence, use this website with caution and make sure to verify the results obtained.
